# Statement of Work: Fernhill Coffee Roasters ecommerce site

**Client:** Fernhill Coffee Roasters (contact: Riley Chen, Head of Digital, riley.chen@fernhillcoffee.example)
**Agency:** Brightline Studio
**Signed:** by both parties

## Objectives

Launch a new online store so Fernhill can sell subscriptions and single bags directly, replacing their marketplace listing.

## Scope

1. **Discovery** (weeks 1-2, starting the first Monday of next month): stakeholder interviews, analytics review, product catalog audit. Deliverable: discovery report.
2. **Design** (weeks 3-6): sitemap, homepage and product page designs, checkout flow. Deliverables: approved designs for homepage, product page and checkout. Design cannot begin until the discovery report is signed off by the client.
3. **Build** (weeks 7-12): storefront build on the client's commerce platform, subscription setup, payment integration. Deliverable: production-ready store.
4. **Launch** (week 13): go-live and two weeks of hypercare.

## Milestones

- Design sign-off: end of week 6. Triggers the second payment.
- Go-live: end of week 13. Triggers the final payment.

## Team

- Brightline: Dana Reyes (engagement lead), Priya Shah (design lead), Lee Kim (developer).
- Fernhill: Riley Chen approves all deliverables. Taylor Brooks (Operations Manager) supplies product data.

## Assumptions

- Fernhill supplies product photography and final product descriptions before the build phase.
- The client's commerce platform license is in place before week 7.

## Out of scope

- Photography, copywriting, and paid advertising.
- Migrating historical order data.

## Fees

Fixed fee of $84,000, paid in three installments: on signing, at design sign-off, and at go-live.
