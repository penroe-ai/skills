# Document shapes

Starting structures for the documents tasks most often call for. Adapt them to the firm's plays and the client's habits. Keep every section. When the facts don't support one, leave its heading with a [TBD] note rather than filling it or dropping it.

## Kickoff agenda

1. Welcome and introductions, with each person's role on the engagement.
2. Why we're doing this: the client's goals, before any deliverables.
3. Scope: what's in, and what's explicitly out.
4. Plan: phases, the first milestone, and key dates.
5. How we'll work: the approval process and who approves, review rounds, how and how often we'll communicate, and who to escalate to.
6. What we need from the client: access, assets, decisions, and by when.
7. Open questions.
8. Next steps, each with an owner and a date.

Give each item a time box. Send the agenda ahead of the meeting.

## Change order

1. **The request:** what's changing, who asked for it and when.
2. **Why:** the business reason.
3. **Impact:** on scope, schedule and cost, and any new risks. Mark anything not yet known as [TBD].
4. **Options:** for example, the same scope at a later date, or the original date with less scope.
5. **Recommendation.**
6. **Approval:** who on the client side can approve it, and that work starts only after approval.

## Handoff note

1. **Context:** the engagement in a paragraph, and where it stands now.
2. **Open work:** tasks, deliverables and their owners and dates, and open risks and issues.
3. **Decisions made,** with their reasons.
4. **People:** client contacts, their roles and how they like to work, and who to ask internally.
5. **Access:** the systems and files the new owner needs. Name them; don't write out passwords or secrets.
6. **Watch out for:** anything the new owner would otherwise learn the hard way.

## Client memo

1. **Bottom line:** the main point or the decision needed, in the first two sentences.
2. **Background:** only what the reader needs to act.
3. **Details or options,** with what each means for them.
4. **Next step:** what happens next, who does it, and when they'll hear from you.

## Meeting recap

1. **Decisions,** with the reason for each.
2. **Actions,** each with an owner and a date.
3. **Open questions,** and who will answer each.
4. **Expected but not done:** anything that was meant to happen and hasn't.

Carry the speakers' hedges through. "We'll try to have it by Friday" stays a try.
